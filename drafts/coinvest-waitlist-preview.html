Straight answers

The objections, taken seriously.

The six things a careful investor asks us first.

If these deals are good, why share them?

Because the buyer leading the deal still has to raise the equity. Many are independent sponsors with no fund behind them. They need partners to close, and people who have run a business are the partners they want.

Buyers pay SourceCo. Whose side are you on?

Fair question. Buyers do pay us to find companies. So every deal says in writing what we earn and from whom, and our own view of the deal lists what worries us next to what we like. Counsel to review.

What happens when a deal goes badly?

Some will. These are small companies, and things break. That is why you set your own amount on every deal, and why nobody here will promise you a return.

How do I know the numbers are real?

You see what the buyer and the lender see: the financials, the diligence work, and the model. Ask anything. If a question cannot be answered, that tells you something too.

What if I never see a deal I like?

Then you never invest. It costs nothing to be a member, and nobody will chase you to put money in.

Why not just buy into a private equity fund?

If you want a manager to choose for you, you should. This is for people who want to see the company first and say yes or no themselves.
