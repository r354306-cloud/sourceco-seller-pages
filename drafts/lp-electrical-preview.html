If you own an electrical company

Will I get lowballed?

Every buyer we introduce is vetted, serious and has the money to close. Each one tells us what it pays a premium for: the kind of work, the revenue mix, the size, the region. We only introduce the ones that fit your company, so you can compare buyers instead of taking the first call. Tell us your mix of service and project work, and we match on it. And there are no broker fees coming out of your price.

How do buyers price an electrical company?

Buyers pay for what the company puts in your pocket each year, not for your sales. Repeat customers, a healthy backlog and a team that runs jobs without you all matter. The same company can be priced very differently by two buyers, so we introduce the ones who fit what you want, and you see how each one counts.

Will I end up working for someone else?

Only if you want to. Tell us how you want it to go: a full exit, staying on to run it, or a handover over months or years. We vet every buyer against that, and against what you want for your team and your brand, and only introduce the ones who agree. Nothing moves without your yes.

Will my electricians stay?

If you want your team to stay, tell us. We only introduce buyers who tell us they keep teams in place, apprentices included, and want to help you grow, with more crews or more territory, for example. You hear from each one before you decide whether to meet.

I hold the master license. What happens when I sell?

Your license is yours and doesn’t go with the company, so the buyer needs a licensed electrician to qualify it in your state. Some buyers keep you on as the license holder for a set time while another electrician steps up. Others already have a licensed electrician in your state. Rules differ by state. Tell us how long you want to stay, and we look for buyers who fit that.

My books are a mess. Can I still talk to you?

Yes. You do not need clean books for the first call. Alia asks a few plain questions: residential, commercial or industrial, service or project work, how many electricians, about how big your backlog is, roughly what you took out last year, and what you want next. Rough numbers are fine. If a buyer fits, you will know what they want to see before anything moves forward.

About SourceCo

How do you make money?

Buyers pay us. Owners pay nothing: no retainer, no success fee, no listing fee. You never pay us anything, at signing or at close.

Are you a broker?

No. A broker lists your company and runs a sale for a fee you pay. We introduce owners to buyers who tell us what they are looking for. We do not list your company, market it or run a sale, and it costs you nothing.

Who sees my name?

Nobody, until you say yes. Buyers see a profile of your company without your name.

Will my electricians, customers or competitors find out?

Nothing is listed or posted. Your name is shared only if you agree to an introduction, so you decide who hears, and when. We only call your cell, never the office.

What happens after I send my details?

Alia from SourceCo calls you and asks what you want for your company and your team. We check it against what buyers are looking for, then tell you which buyers fit and why. Nothing is shared with anyone while we do that.

Do I have to meet anyone?

No. For each buyer we send you who they are and why they fit. You choose whether to meet.

What if my company is small, or I am not ready?

Most buyers on SourceCo look for companies with $1.2M+ in revenue, and owners who are only exploring are welcome. If no buyer fits, we say so and point you to where to look. If you are not ready, choose “Just exploring”. Alia will still call to talk about what buyers in electrical contracting look for and how they would value your company.
