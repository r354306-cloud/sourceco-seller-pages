One real deal per trade finishes HVAC and electrical.

The slot is styled to match Andy’s story, so it drops straight in. Here’s what it needs:

Owner name and titleFounder, Owner, President
Company and citye.g. Smith Heating, Tulsa, OK
Buyer and month closedWho bought it, and when
A one or two line quoteWhat SourceCo did differently
What happened to the teame.g. every tech kept their job
A photoOptional. Initials fill in without one
